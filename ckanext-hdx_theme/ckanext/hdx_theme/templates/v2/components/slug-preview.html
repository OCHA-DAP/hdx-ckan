{# Snippet: slug-preview.html Path: templates/v2/components/slug-preview.html Renders a c-slug-preview — a URL slug field (text-field.html) and a "URL: [Edit]" preview row. With JS (slug-preview.js, page-loaded `v2-slug-preview-scripts`) the preview replaces the field unless the field has errors, the slug follows the `target_id` field while that field was empty at load and the slug hasn't been typed into, and Edit (or a failed native `required` check) reveals the field. Without JS only the field renders.
