{# Snippet: date-picker.html Path: templates/v2/components/date-picker.html Renders a c-date-picker — a select-like button that opens a calendar panel (built on the c-dropdown-calendar panel styles) and posts the chosen date as yyyy-mm-dd in a hidden input. The button shows the date as "November 16, 2026". The panel shows a month's day grid with previous / next month; its title switches to a 12-month grid with previous / next year. Keyboard: arrows move by day / week, Home / End to the week's start / end, Page Up / Down by month (Shift: by year), Enter / Space choose, Escape closes. Days outside min / max are disabled. JS components/date-picker.js; control it with window.hdxV2DatePicker(rootEl).setValue(iso) / .setMin(iso) / .setMax(iso) / .setDisabled(bool). Choosing a date dispatches a bubbling `date-picker:change` event (detail.value = yyyy-mm-dd).
